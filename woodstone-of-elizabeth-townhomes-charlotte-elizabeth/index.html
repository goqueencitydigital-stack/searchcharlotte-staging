About the Elizabeth area

Elizabeth is one of Charlotte's oldest in-town neighborhoods, just east of Uptown. It grew around an 1891 streetcar line and takes its name from Elizabeth College, a women's college once located where Novant Health Presbyterian Medical Center now stands. Elizabeth is home to Independence Park, Charlotte's first public park.
