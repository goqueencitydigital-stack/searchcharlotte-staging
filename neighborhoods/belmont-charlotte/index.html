Neighborhood Guide

Living in Belmont (Charlotte)

Belmont is a historic neighborhood just northeast of Uptown Charlotte, between Villa Heights and the Plaza Midwood area. It should not be confused with the separate City of Belmont in Gaston County, roughly 12 miles west. Belmont developed in the early 20th century, and its grid of small lots still reflects that era.

Housing is a mix of early-1900s cottages and bungalows, many renovated, and newer infill homes and townhomes that have been built on vacant or redeveloped lots. The neighborhood is close to Optimist Hall, the Parkwood LYNX Blue Line station, and the restaurants of Plaza Midwood and NoDa. Uptown is a short drive or bike ride away. Most addresses fall in ZIP code 28205, with some in 28206. Buyers should expect a mix of renovated originals and new construction on the same block, which can widen the range of prices and lot sizes.

Homes & housing

Typical housing: Early-20th-century mill cottages and bungalows; Modern infill single-family homes; Townhomes.

Getting around

Main routes and transit: LYNX Blue Line – Parkwood station (nearby); The Plaza; Parkwood Ave; I-277.
