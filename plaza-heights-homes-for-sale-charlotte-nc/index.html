About the NoDa area

NoDa, short for North Davidson, is Charlotte's best-known arts district, centered on North Davidson Street and 36th Street about two and a half miles northeast of Uptown. It occupies the historic North Charlotte mill village, which grew around textile mills in the early 1900s and is listed on the National Register of Historic Places.
