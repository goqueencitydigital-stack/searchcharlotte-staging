Charlotte housing market,
August 2026

A plain-language summary of Canopy Realtor® Association's August 2026 figures. Every number below was published by Canopy; nothing is estimated.

Figures retrieved 2026-10-28 · Canopy may revise recent months as MLS data updates

Median sales price$410,000up 2.5% from a year earlier
Closed sales3,560down 8.7% from a year earlier
New listings4,940down 2.2% from a year earlier
Pending sales3,763down 1.8% from a year earlier

Across Canopy's Charlotte region, the median sales price was $410,000 (up 2.5% from a year earlier). Closed sales totaled 3,560 (down 8.7% from a year earlier). New listings came to 4,940 (down 2.2% from a year earlier). Pending sales were 3,763 (down 1.8% from a year earlier). Inventory stood at 13,574 (up 7.1% from a year earlier). Months of supply was 3.7 months (up 5.7% from a year earlier). Homes spent 55 days on market before an accepted offer (up 17.0% from a year earlier). Sellers received 95.5% of original list price (up 0.2% from a year earlier (computed from published values)).

In Mecklenburg County, the median sales price was $468,000 (up 1.8% from a year earlier). Closed sales totaled 1,230 (down 6.4% from a year earlier). New listings came to 1,648 (down 4.5% from a year earlier). Pending sales were 1,232 (down 8.1% from a year earlier). Inventory stood at 4,543 (up 12.7% from a year earlier). Months of supply was 3.6 months (up 24.1% from a year earlier (computed from published values)). Homes spent 45 days on market before an accepted offer. Sellers received 96.1% of original list price (down 0.1% from a year earlier (computed from published values)).

Within the City of Charlotte, the median sales price was $427,500 (up 0.6% from a year earlier). Closed sales totaled 971 (down 5.3% from a year earlier). New listings came to 1,363 (up 0.9% from a year earlier). Pending sales were 983 (down 4.4% from a year earlier). Inventory stood at 3,737 (up 15.7% from a year earlier). Months of supply was 3.7 months (up 27.6% from a year earlier (computed from published values)). Homes spent 47 days on market before an accepted offer (up 6.8% from a year earlier (computed from published values)). Sellers received 95.9% of original list price (down 0.2% from a year earlier (computed from published values)).

Among areas with at least 50 closed sales, Lake Norman, NC had the largest year-over-year change in median price (+29.0%, to $690,000), while Lake Wylie had the weakest (-13.9%, to $584,000). Monthly medians in smaller areas move around, so a single month is not a trend.
