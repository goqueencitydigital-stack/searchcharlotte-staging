About the Rea Farms area

Rea Farms is a mixed-use development in south Charlotte near Providence Road and Ardrey Kell Road, close to I-485. Built on the roughly 188-acre site of the former Charlotte Golf Links, with construction beginning in 2017, it combines a village center of shops, restaurants, and offices with apartments, townhomes, and single-family homes.
