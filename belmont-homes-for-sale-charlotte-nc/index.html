About the Belmont (Charlotte) area

Belmont is a historic neighborhood just northeast of Uptown Charlotte, between Villa Heights and the Plaza Midwood area. It should not be confused with the separate City of Belmont in Gaston County, roughly 12 miles west. Belmont developed in the early 20th century, and its grid of small lots still reflects that era.
