About the Pineville area

Pineville is a small town at the southern tip of Mecklenburg County, between Charlotte and Fort Mill, South Carolina. Its population was 10,602 at the 2020 census, up from 7,479 in 2010. The town has a small historic downtown along Main Street and is best known regionally for Carolina Place mall and the retail corridors around it.
