Charlotte housing market,
July 2026

A plain-language summary of Canopy Realtor® Association's July 2026 figures. Every number below was published by Canopy; nothing is estimated.

Figures retrieved 2026-10-28 · Canopy may revise recent months as MLS data updates

Median sales price$410,000up 1.1% from a year earlier
Closed sales4,142up 1.6% from a year earlier
New listings5,532up 1.1% from a year earlier
Pending sales3,963up 2.1% from a year earlier

Across Canopy's Charlotte region, the median sales price was $410,000 (up 1.1% from a year earlier). Closed sales totaled 4,142 (up 1.6% from a year earlier). New listings came to 5,532 (up 1.1% from a year earlier). Pending sales were 3,963 (up 2.1% from a year earlier). Inventory stood at 13,600 (up 6.9% from a year earlier). Months of supply was 3.7 months (up 5.7% from a year earlier). Homes spent 55 days on market before an accepted offer (up 19.6% from a year earlier). Sellers received 96.0% of original list price (up 0.1% from a year earlier (computed from published values)).

In Mecklenburg County, the median sales price was $468,500 (up 2.0% from a year earlier). Closed sales totaled 1,470 (up 3.4% from a year earlier). New listings came to 1,911 (up 7.4% from a year earlier). Pending sales were 1,322 (down 0.3% from a year earlier). Inventory stood at 4,593 (up 13.1% from a year earlier). Months of supply was 3.6 months (up 20.0% from a year earlier (computed from published values)). Homes spent 40 days on market before an accepted offer (up 8.1% from a year earlier (computed from published values)). Sellers received 96.5% of original list price (down 0.4% from a year earlier (computed from published values)).

Within the City of Charlotte, the median sales price was $430,000 (down 2.3% from a year earlier). Closed sales totaled 1,170 (up 2.4% from a year earlier). New listings came to 1,540 (up 8.5% from a year earlier). Pending sales were 1,041 (up 0.7% from a year earlier). Inventory stood at 3,754 (up 15.8% from a year earlier). Months of supply was 3.7 months (up 27.6% from a year earlier (computed from published values)). Homes spent 40 days on market before an accepted offer (up 8.1% from a year earlier (computed from published values)). Sellers received 96.5% of original list price (down 0.5% from a year earlier (computed from published values)).

Among areas with at least 50 closed sales, Lake Norman, NC had the largest year-over-year change in median price (+29.5%, to $700,000), while Waxhaw, NC had the weakest (-14.7%, to $657,000). Monthly medians in smaller areas move around, so a single month is not a trend.
