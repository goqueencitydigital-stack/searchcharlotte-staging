Methodology

How the numbers are calculated

Effective gross income
(Monthly rent + other income) × 12 × (1 − vacancy %).
Operating expenses
Property tax (price × rate ÷ 100) + insurance + HOA + maintenance (% of value) + management (% of collected income) + capital reserves (% of scheduled rent) + other expenses. Mortgage payments are not operating expenses.
Net operating income (NOI)
Effective gross income − operating expenses.
Annual debt service
12 × the fixed principal-and-interest payment on (price × (1 − down %)) at your rate and term.
Cash flow
NOI − annual debt service, before income taxes and depreciation.
Cap rate
NOI ÷ purchase price.
Cash-on-cash return
Annual cash flow ÷ cash invested (down payment + closing costs + upfront repairs).
DSCR
NOI ÷ annual debt service. Below 1.0 means NOI does not cover the mortgage payment. Lenders that underwrite on DSCR set their own minimums and may use a different rent figure.
