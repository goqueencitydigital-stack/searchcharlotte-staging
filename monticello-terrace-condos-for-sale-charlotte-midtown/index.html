About the Cherry area

Cherry is one of Charlotte's oldest neighborhoods, located just southeast of Uptown near Midtown. It is a historically African-American neighborhood, developed in the late 1800s and early 1900s, and it is bounded by Little Sugar Creek, Kenilworth Avenue, the John Belk Freeway (I-277), East 4th Street, Queens Road, and Henley Place.
