About the Belmont area

Belmont is a small suburban city in Gaston County, about 9 miles east of Gastonia and just across the Catawba River from western Mecklenburg County. Its population was 15,010 at the 2020 census. The city should not be confused with the Belmont neighborhood inside Charlotte.
