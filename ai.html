How it works

Your words in.
MLS filters out.

  1. You describe itBudget, beds, style, garage, yard, areas, drive time to work, places you want nearby — typed or spoken.
  2. It becomes filtersEach request becomes a removable chip: a price limit, a 3-car garage, a 20-minute drive to Uptown. You can see exactly what's being searched.
  3. The search does the restHomes come only from the listing search. If nothing matches, it says so and shows the tradeoffs.

Disclosure. Search Charlotte AI turns your words into MLS search filters. It never creates listings, prices or property facts, and it doesn't rank homes or areas by safety, schools or who lives there. Drive times are straight-line estimates, not routes. Recommendations compare only budget, sample listing prices, drive time, nearby place counts (OpenStreetMap) and home types. A Search Charlotte agent confirms every showing request.
