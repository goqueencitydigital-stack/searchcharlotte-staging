About the Blakeney area

Blakeney is a south Charlotte community centered on the Blakeney shopping center near Rea Road and I-485. The area combines retail, restaurants, and surrounding residential neighborhoods developed mostly in the 2000s, giving residents a nearby hub for groceries, dining, and services.
