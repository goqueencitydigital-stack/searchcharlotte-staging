Monthly market report

Charlotte housing market,
September 2026

A plain-language summary of Canopy Realtor® Association's September 2026 figures. Every number below was published by Canopy; nothing is estimated.

Figures retrieved 2026-10-28 · Canopy may revise recent months as MLS data updates

Median sales price$392,990down 0.5% from a year earlier
Closed sales3,439down 10.1% from a year earlier
New listings4,674down 7.8% from a year earlier
Pending sales3,370down 4.5% from a year earlier

Across Canopy's Charlotte region, the median sales price was $392,990 (down 0.5% from a year earlier). Closed sales totaled 3,439 (down 10.1% from a year earlier). New listings came to 4,674 (down 7.8% from a year earlier). Pending sales were 3,370 (down 4.5% from a year earlier). Homes spent 54 days on market before an accepted offer (unchanged from a year earlier). Sellers received 95.1% of original list price (unchanged from a year earlier).

Canopy did not publish region-wide homes for sale (inventory) or months supply of inventory for September 2026 in the sources we collected, so we leave those figures out rather than estimate them.

In Mecklenburg County, the median sales price was $450,000 (unchanged from a year earlier). Closed sales totaled 1,129 (down 15.6% from a year earlier). New listings came to 1,662 (down 5.0% from a year earlier). Pending sales were 1,077 (down 12.4% from a year earlier). Inventory stood at 4,622 (up 12.6% from a year earlier). Months of supply was 3.7 months (up 23.3% from a year earlier (computed from published values)). Homes spent 51 days on market before an accepted offer (up 8.5% from a year earlier). Sellers received 95.5% of original list price (down 0.6% from a year earlier (computed from published values)).

Within the City of Charlotte, the median sales price was $420,000 (up 0.5% from a year earlier). Closed sales totaled 875 (down 16.2% from a year earlier). New listings came to 1,364 (down 2.2% from a year earlier). Pending sales were 860 (down 9.7% from a year earlier). Inventory stood at 3,827 (up 15.5% from a year earlier). Months of supply was 3.8 months (up 26.7% from a year earlier (computed from published values)). Homes spent 49 days on market before an accepted offer (up 6.5% from a year earlier (computed from published values)). Sellers received 95.5% of original list price (down 0.6% from a year earlier (computed from published values)).

Among areas with at least 50 closed sales, Belmont, NC had the largest year-over-year change in median price (+25.3%, to $550,000), while Huntersville, NC had the weakest (-15.6%, to $539,950). Monthly medians in smaller areas move around, so a single month is not a trend.
