Monthly market report

Charlotte housing market,
October 2026

A plain-language summary of Canopy Realtor® Association's October 2026 figures. Every number below was published by Canopy; nothing is estimated.

Figures retrieved 2026-11-12 · Canopy may revise recent months as MLS data updates

Median sales price$390,000down 2.5% from a year earlier
Closed sales3,134down 16.0% from a year earlier
New listings4,787down 3.3% from a year earlier
Homes for sale14,096up 9.6% from a year earlier

Across Canopy's Charlotte region, the median sales price was $390,000 (down 2.5% from a year earlier). Closed sales totaled 3,134 (down 16.0% from a year earlier). New listings came to 4,787 (down 3.3% from a year earlier). Pending sales were 3,055 (down 11.4% from a year earlier). Inventory stood at 14,096 homes (up 9.6% from a year earlier). Months supply of inventory was 4.0 (up 11.1% from a year earlier). Homes spent 57 days on market until sale (up 5.6% from a year earlier). Sellers received 94.8% of original list price (down 0.2% from a year earlier).

In Mecklenburg County, the median sales price was $450,000 (down 0.6% from a year earlier). Closed sales totaled 1,060 (down 18.8% from a year earlier). New listings came to 1,662 (down 1.3% from a year earlier). Pending sales were 1,001 (down 17.0% from a year earlier). Inventory stood at 4,775 homes (up 16.0% from a year earlier). Months supply of inventory was 3.9 (up 21.9% from a year earlier). Homes spent 52 days on market until sale (up 6.1% from a year earlier). Sellers received 95.1% of original list price (down 0.7% from a year earlier).

Within the City of Charlotte, the median sales price was $416,355 (down 2.0% from a year earlier). Closed sales totaled 824 (down 19.0% from a year earlier). New listings came to 1,335 (down 3.0% from a year earlier). Pending sales were 793 (down 17.7% from a year earlier). Inventory stood at 3,950 homes (up 18.4% from a year earlier). Months supply of inventory was 4.0 (up 21.2% from a year earlier). Homes spent 51 days on market until sale (up 4.1% from a year earlier). Sellers received 95.1% of original list price (down 0.6% from a year earlier).

Among areas with at least 50 closed sales, Waxhaw had the largest year-over-year change in median price (+7.4%, to $727,450), while Lincoln County had the weakest (-12.5%, to $400,000). Monthly medians in smaller areas move around, so a single month is not a trend.

Median and average prices do not account for sale concessions or down payment assistance, per Canopy's footnote.
