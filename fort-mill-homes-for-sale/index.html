About the Fort Mill area

Fort Mill is a town in York County, South Carolina, just south of the North Carolina line. Its population was 24,521 at the 2020 census. Some businesses and residents in Indian Land, in neighboring Lancaster County, share a Fort Mill mailing address, but the town boundary itself lies within York County.
