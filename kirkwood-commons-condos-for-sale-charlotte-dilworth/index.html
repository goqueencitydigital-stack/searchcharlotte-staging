About the Dilworth area

Dilworth was Charlotte's first streetcar suburb, established in the 1890s by Edward Dilworth Latta on land southwest of the original city limits. The neighborhood was planned around Latta Park, designed by Joseph Forsyth Johnson, and much of Dilworth is now part of a local historic district.
