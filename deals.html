Deal Radar

Listing activity worth a second look

Price reductions, homes back on market, fresh listings, open houses and builder incentives — signals you can verify in the listing record. No "undervalued" guesses.

Create a deal alertHow Deal Radar works

Sample data — live MLS data via Showcase IDX at launch. Price and status history shown here is demo data generated for the sample listings.

Methodology

Signals, not opinions

Every flag on Deal Radar comes from a fact in the listing record that you or your agent can check.

Price Reduced / ×N
The list price is lower than the original list price; ×N counts recorded price changes. Shows the dollar difference.
Back on Market
The listing went pending or under contract and returned to active status.
New Listing
Listed within the last 7 days, or flagged new by the listing agent.
Open House
An open house is scheduled in the listing record. Times can change — confirm before you go.
Builder Incentive
The listing remarks advertise a builder incentive. Terms are set by the builder; confirm in writing.
Long days on market
45 or more days on market. Longer market time can mean more room to negotiate — or a reason to look closer.

Why there's no "undervalued" label

Calling a home below market requires licensed closed-sale data (Canopy MLS sold comps) and an analysis by a licensed professional. A price drop isn't proof of a bargain, and a high price isn't proof of a bad deal. At launch, a "below recent comparable sales" comparison may be added only from licensed closed-sale data with its method shown — never as an appraisal. Ask an agent for a comparative market analysis.
