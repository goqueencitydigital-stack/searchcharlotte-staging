About the Lansdowne area

Lansdowne is an established southeast Charlotte neighborhood near the Providence Road and Sardis Road corridors, east of SouthPark. Most homes were built in the 1960s and 1970s on wooded lots, and the neighborhood has kept a quiet, residential feel.
