Frequently
asked questions

Straight answers about buying a home in Charlotte.

Get pre-approved with a lender so you know your budget, then set up a saved search for the areas you like. A Search Charlotte agent can tour homes with you, pull recent sales, and write your offer when you find the one.
It depends on the loan. Some conventional loans allow as little as 3% down, FHA loans can start at 3.5%, and VA and USDA loans may require no down payment for eligible buyers. Your lender will confirm what you qualify for.
Searching can take days or months. Once you're under contract, a financed purchase typically closes in about 30–45 days. In North Carolina, the due-diligence period is when you complete inspections and appraisal.
Buyers usually pay lender fees, title insurance, attorney fees (North Carolina closings are handled by an attorney), recording fees and prepaid taxes and insurance. Your lender's Loan Estimate lists the exact figures.
The best fit depends on your budget, commute and the lifestyle you want, walkable streets in Dilworth and South End, larger lots in Mint Hill and Union County, or lake living around Lake Norman. Try the neighborhood finder above or browse our neighborhood guides.
