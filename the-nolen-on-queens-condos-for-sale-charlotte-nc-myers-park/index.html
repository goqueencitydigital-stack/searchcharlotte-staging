About the Myers Park area

Myers Park is one of Charlotte's best-known historic neighborhoods, developed beginning in 1911 on the former John Springs Myers farm. It was planned by landscape architect John Nolen, whose design gave the neighborhood its curving streets, large lots, and the tree-lined boulevards of Queens Road. Myers Park is listed as a historic district.
