About the Madison Park area

Madison Park is a south Charlotte neighborhood located between South Boulevard, East Woodlawn Road, and Park Road, extending slightly beyond Tyvola Road. It is often grouped with the Montford Drive and Park Road Shopping Center area, and an active neighborhood association helps define its boundaries.
