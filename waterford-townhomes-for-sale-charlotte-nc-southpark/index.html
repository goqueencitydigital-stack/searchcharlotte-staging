About the SouthPark area

SouthPark is an edge city about six miles south of Uptown, centered at the intersection of Fairview Road and Sharon Road. Its name comes from SouthPark Mall, which opened in 1970 and is the largest shopping mall in the Carolinas. The district is also one of Charlotte's major business centers.
