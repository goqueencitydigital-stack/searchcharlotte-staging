About the Ballantyne area

Ballantyne is a roughly 2,000-acre planned district at Charlotte's southern edge, next to the South Carolina state line. It grew up around the Ballantyne Corporate Park and the Ballantyne Hotel starting in the 1990s, and it now mixes office campuses, apartments, townhome communities, and established single-family subdivisions. Recent redevelopment has added a public park and amphitheater area at the former corporate campus core.
