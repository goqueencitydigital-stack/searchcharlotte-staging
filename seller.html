No automated home value. We don't show an instant or algorithmic estimate of your home's value. Your price comes from a comparative market analysis (CMA) that a licensed agent prepares using recent closed sales, your home's condition and current competition. A CMA is not an appraisal.

Step 1

Request a CMA

Tell us about the property. A licensed agent reviews recent closed sales nearby and sends you a pricing range with the comparables behind it, usually within one business day.

  • Closed sales from Canopy MLS, adjusted for size, condition and features
  • Active and pending competition in your area
  • A suggested list-price range and the reasoning for it
  • No cost and no obligation to list

Thanks. A licensed agent will review your property and follow up within one business day. Your CMA will be prepared by a person, not an algorithm.
